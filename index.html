Privacy Policy

App: Bottlemark  ·  Developer: Small Tools Studio  ·  Last updated: May 10, 2026

Bottlemark ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal wine note app built and operated by Small Tools Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the core functionality of Bottlemark:

  • Account information: Your email address and password, used to create and authenticate your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Wine notes: Data you enter into the app, including wine names, ratings, prices, purchase locations, drink dates, and personal notes.
  • Photos: Wine label photos you choose to upload, stored via Firebase Storage.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the Bottlemark service:

  • To authenticate your account and keep your notes private and secure.
  • To store and display your wine notes across sessions and devices.
  • To allow you to add, edit, and delete your own records.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Bottlemark uses Google Firebase to store your data securely:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your email/password login.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your wine notes.
  • Firebase Storage — stores photos you upload.

Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Your data is stored on Google's servers. For more information, see Firebase's Privacy and Security.

We do not share your data with any other third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You may delete individual wine notes at any time within the app. To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at the email below and we will process your request within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored in your account (visible within the app).
  • Edit or delete any wine notes you have created.
  • Request full deletion of your account and all associated data.

6. Children's Privacy

Bottlemark is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We rely on Firebase's industry-standard security infrastructure to protect your data. Access to your notes is restricted to your own account. We do not have access to your password.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your Bottlemark account and all associated data, please send an email to slow3605@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request and permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days. Please note that once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
